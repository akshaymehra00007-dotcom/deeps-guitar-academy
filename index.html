The learning path

Built around the way
you want to play.

Whether you're picking up a guitar for the first time or trying to become a more confident player, your learning should feel structured, musical and motivating.

01 / BEGINNER

Start from zero

Build the essentials: posture, chords, rhythm, transitions and the habits that make progress feel natural.

02 / SKILL

Level up your playing

Go beyond basic chords with stronger rhythm, technique, song application and a deeper understanding of the fretboard.

03 / PERFORMANCE

Play with confidence

Turn practice into performance. Develop timing, musicality and the confidence to play for an audience.
